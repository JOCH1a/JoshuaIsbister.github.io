Embedded Step Tracker Watch

Designed, developed, assembled and tested a wearable step-tracking device built around an accelerometer and MCU — including the signal chain, power delivery, mechanical enclosure, display, and user interface.

Altium STM32 LTspice Sallen-Key Filter
PCB4-layer, Altium
Filter2nd-order Butterworth
PowerBuck + LDO
StatusBuilt

Overview

A wrist-worn step tracker built end-to-end: from accelerometer signal conditioning through to a finished, wearable enclosure. The brief required every subsystem — sensing, filtering, power, display, and interface — to work together on one board.

Design & approach

  • Designed a 4-layer PCB in Altium carrying the accelerometer, MCU header, display driver, status indicators, and button interfaces
  • Built a 2nd-order Butterworth active low-pass filter using a Sallen-Key topology to clean the accelerometer signal, simulated in LTspice before committing to hardware
  • Designed onboard power delivery using a buck converter paired with an LDO to supply clean, regulated rails from a single battery input
  • Hand-assembled the board using a mix of through-hole, SMD, and hotplate soldering, then wrote embedded C/C++ on STM32 to read, filter, and display step counts in real time

Results

A fully assembled, working device — signal chain, power system, and firmware all validated on hardware, housed in a custom mechanical enclosure.
