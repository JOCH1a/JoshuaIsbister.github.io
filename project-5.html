UTS Motorsports Seat — FSAE

Designed and manufactured a composite carbon fibre / fibreglass driver's seat for the UTS Motorsports FSAE car.

SolidWorks Composites Mold Design Surface Modelling
MaterialCarbon fibre / fibreglass
ProcessWet layup, vacuum bag
CADSurface modelling
StatusBuilt

Overview

A composite driver's seat designed and manufactured for the UTS Motorsports FSAE car, covering the full path from CAD surfacing through to a finished, installed part.

Design & approach

  • Surface-modelled the seat geometry in SolidWorks to fit driver ergonomics and chassis mounting points
  • Designed the mold, including draft angle analysis to ensure clean part release
  • Manufactured using composite layup techniques: mold preparation, vacuum bagging, wet layup, and post-processing

Results

A finished composite seat, manufactured and fitted to the FSAE car.
