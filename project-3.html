Overview

A full kinematic simulation of a 6-DOF industrial robot arm, built to safely operate alongside two other robot players and a robot dealer in a simulated game of blackjack — a proxy for real collaborative-robot deployment.

Design & approach

  • Applied collaborative robot practices — safety risk assessments, force and motion damping, and speed management — throughout the design
  • Implemented forward and inverse kinematics to drive the arm to target poses
  • Used resolved motion rate control (RMRC) for smooth, continuous trajectory tracking
  • Built collision detection between the arm, other robots, and the environment
  • Applied damped least squares to handle motion near kinematic singularities
  • Modelled and programmed the full system in Python within a Swift environment simulation

Results

A working simulated multi-robot environment where the arm could safely reach, manipulate, and interact without collisions, even near singular configurations.
