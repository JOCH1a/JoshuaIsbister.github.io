FDM Printed Pliers Challenge

Competed against 116 other students to design the highest strength-to-weight-ratio pliers printed from PLA, using FEA, topology optimisation, and additive manufacturing design principles.

Overview

A faculty-wide design challenge to produce the strongest possible pliers, by weight, printable on an FDM machine in PLA — placing 3rd out of 116 competing students.

Design & approach

  • Used FEA to identify and reduce material in low-stress regions of the design
  • Applied topology optimisation to maximise strength-to-weight ratio within the PLA/FDM constraints
  • Designed for additive manufacturing — print orientation, layer strength, and support minimisation

Results

Placed 3rd of 116 entries, achieving a strength-to-weight ratio of 1.17 kgf/g — the final part weighed 22.2 g and withstood 25.8 kgf of force before breaking.
